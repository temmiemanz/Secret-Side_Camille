Build animate humanoid minions from scrap, junk, or dead organic material by hand. Every piece bonds instantly on contact — only the user can remove them. Minions understand user intent perfectly without words. Not smart, but completely loyal and accurate. Fallen minions can be cannibalized. No mental toll.
Valid minion requires
One head and a torso
At least two arms and at least two legs
Every piece must be something the user can lift themselves

Living tissue does not respond — dead organic material only.
Minions can't scout or operate independently — they go where the user goes.
Lost limbs don't regenerate — must be physically reattached.
Leaving a 5ft radius from attached material causes it to lose adhesion.

Camille's mother, Rose, died when he was eight — killed during a trade gone wrong, trying to secure medicine for the hemophilia she and his father Alexios both carried. She managed to take down the person who stabbed her before she bled out. His father heard the commotion. Camille followed. He still remembers the floor.
After that, Alex kept them moving — never staying anywhere long enough to become a target. Eventually they found a hut in the woods and Alex made it a home, spending years teaching Camille how to take care of himself. It wasn't much, but it was theirs.
When Camille was fourteen, Alex came home with a cut that hemophilia and poor supplies turned into a fatal infection. Camille found a traveling medical group led by a man named Erick and came back with them — but came back too late. Alex died that night. Camille asked Erick if he could join before he finished crying.
The next four years were stability. He and Tucker became inseparable — learning medicine, moving with the group, helping anyone who asked. Then a group asked for help and led them into an ambush. Erick, Tucker, all of them. Gone. Camille hid and watched and couldn't do anything. He tried to go back to medical work after. He couldn't. The sight of blood made him physically sick. What he found he could do was clean things. Organize things. Keep his hands moving. The lab coat stays on. Everything has a place.
